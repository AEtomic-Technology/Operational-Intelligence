# Operational Intelligence

*A research programme by Aetomic Technology.*

---

## Overview

Operational Intelligence is a long-term research programme examining how organisations perceive, interpret and respond to changing operational conditions.

Rather than beginning with software, this repository begins with first principles.

It explores the concepts, models and reasoning that underpin effective operational decision-making across security, facilities management, critical infrastructure, government, professional services and other operationally intensive environments.

The research documented here informs the design and development of Aetomic's Operational Intelligence technologies, including the Lightbringer Operational Intelligence Engine and its associated applications.

---

## Purpose

The objective of this repository is to develop a practical body of knowledge on Operational Intelligence.

The work focuses on questions such as:

* How do organisations maintain operational stability?
* How do experienced operators recognise meaningful change?
* What distinguishes information from operationally significant signals?
* How should evidence, context and authority influence decision-making?
* How can organisational knowledge be preserved, reasoned upon and applied consistently?
* What principles should guide the design of trustworthy Operational Intelligence systems?

These papers are intended to be evidence-based, practical and grounded in operational experience rather than technology trends.

## Research Principles

This repository is guided by several principles:

* Begin with operational reality before proposing technological solutions.
* Prefer evidence over assumption.
* Preserve context alongside data.
* Recognise that technology should strengthen human judgement rather than replace it.
* Develop concepts that remain useful across industries and operational domains.
* Build cumulative knowledge through disciplined research and continuous refinement.
