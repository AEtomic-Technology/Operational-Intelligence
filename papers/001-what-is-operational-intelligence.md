# Paper 001: What Is Operational Intelligence?

**Status:** Draft v0.1
**Author:** Roy Phang
**Publisher:** Aetomic Technology Research
**Research Programme:** Operational Intelligence

---

## Abstract

Operational Intelligence is frequently described as the collection, analysis and presentation of operational data to support organisational decision-making. This paper argues that such definitions are incomplete.

Operational Intelligence is not simply the ability to process information more quickly. It is the organisational capability to perceive changing operational conditions, interpret their significance within context and support timely, proportionate and accountable action.

This paper establishes the foundational definition of Operational Intelligence and introduces the conceptual framework that underpins the broader Operational Intelligence research programme.

---

## Research Questions

This paper explores several fundamental questions:

* What is Operational Intelligence?
* Why do existing definitions fail to describe how experienced operators actually make decisions?
* What distinguishes Operational Intelligence from business intelligence, automation and general-purpose artificial intelligence?
* Why should Operational Intelligence begin with operational reality rather than technology?
